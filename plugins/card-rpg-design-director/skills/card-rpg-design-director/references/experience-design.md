# Experience Design Modes

Use this reference for game-concept review, core-loop design, and MDA analysis.

## Game concept review

Identify:

- the player fantasy and the meaningful decisions that express it;
- the target experience and likely audience;
- the differentiator from adjacent card-building RPGs;
- the minimum proof that the concept is worth expanding;
- scope or dependency risks that are unusually costly for a solo developer.

Judge the concept as a hypothesis, not a pitch. Prefer a falsifiable prototype question over a broad claim that the idea is fun.

## Core-loop design

Map only the loops relevant to the request:

- moment-to-moment decisions;
- combat encounter loop;
- run or mission loop;
- long-term progression loop.

For each loop, identify input, player decision, system response, feedback, reward, and the reason to repeat. Check whether rewards merely increase numbers or create new decisions. Flag loops that depend on future content volume before their basic interaction is satisfying.

## MDA analysis

Trace in this order:

1. `Mechanics`: explicit rules and available actions.
2. `Dynamics`: behavior likely to emerge when mechanics interact.
3. `Aesthetics`: intended player feelings and motivations.

Then trace backward from the desired experience to the minimum mechanics that could plausibly produce it. Mark speculative dynamics and define a playtest observation that could confirm or reject them.
